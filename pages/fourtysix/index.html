46er

This page is best for tracking my 46 High Peaks completion progress. Try out printing the page to track yourself! I'd recommend checking out Hiking and Climbing for the gear that I use. I've split the 46 mountins into 23 groups, they are grouped by adjacent color. Look at the table at the bottom for coordinates of the start location. Not everything here is perfectly correct.

It's important to have fun but remember "It's mandatory to make it down, making it up is optional", so don't be scared turning around. Make sure to have a map, either a physical print out or a offline map through Hiiker. Be prepared with good equipment that you've used before and are comfortable with.
